## Overview: The Builder Platform

Bringing a physical hardware product to market has traditionally required deep venture capital connections, fragmented tooling (spread across spreadsheets, Discord, GitHub, and Jira), opaque equity promises, and complex offshore manufacturing knowledge.

**Qatoto** replaces this broken workflow with an integrated, end-to-end execution system. Founders with an idea can match with skilled engineers and researchers, lock in deterministic dynamic equity before work starts, log daily progress that is automatically audited against real code and CAD artifacts, and transition seamlessly into manufacturing, global storefront distribution, and fulfillment.

Today, this bottleneck is equally severe for modern, multi-disciplinary ventures:

- **Researchers and scientists** (biologists, chemists, materials experts) frequently make discoveries that stay trapped in academic journals because they lack software engineers, operational talent, or commercial pipelines to turn them into deployed tools.
- **Software engineers** want to build high-impact technologies that solve urgent real-world problems, but often lack verified domain problem mapping or transparent equity structures.
- **Hardware creators** encounter severe supply chain friction, opaque manufacturing barriers, and premature scaling costs.
- **Visionaries** who see a critical problem facing everyday citizens struggle to coordinate multi-disciplinary teams across software, wet-lab science, and engineering.

**Qatoto provides the unifying pipeline for builders.** Whether you are writing distributed software, synthesizing novel filtration chemistry, engineering diagnostic hardware, or coordinating an entire civic venture, Qatoto provides the demand signals, collaborative research branches, transparent equity ledger, and automated build tracking needed to deliver solutions end-to-end.

```
│ 01. PITCH / MAP │ ──> │  02. ASSEMBLE   │ ──> │   03. RAISE     │ ──> │   04. BUILD     │ ──> │  05. DEPLOY     │
│ Civic problem   │     │ Cross-domain    │     │ Milestone-      │     │ Daily logs, AST │     │ Store, field    │
│ & open research │     │ software/sci/hw │     │ backed capital  │     │ diffs & lab POE │     │ rollout & studio│
└─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘
```

## Anchored in Everyday Public Problems

At the heart of Qatoto is **Civic Pulse** (`/research-and-development/problem-map`), a community-powered problem discovery engine. Rather than inventing artificial startup concepts, builders on Qatoto begin by exploring verified, geo-tagged problems reported by everyday citizens worldwide:

- **Water & Sanitation**: Contaminated aquifers, heavy metal leaching, broken municipal distribution lines.
- **Energy & Clean Utilities**: Unreliable micro-grids, high-cost battery storage, diesel generator dependency.
- **Agriculture & Food Preservation**: Cold-chain failures causing post-harvest crop loss, pest outbreaks, soil nutrient depletion.
- **Healthcare & Diagnostics**: Lack of decentralized point-of-care diagnostics, refrigerated medicine spoilage, remote clinic supply shortages.
- **Infrastructure, Housing & Transport**: Unpaved access roads, structural insulation failures, localized transit gridlocks.
- **Digital Infrastructure & Open Tools**: Missing open-source software, inaccessible communication networks, localized data collection deficits.

Visionaries analyze these real-world pain points, inspect statistical density clusters, and rally builders across software, biology, chemistry, and hardware to construct comprehensive solutions.

## For Builders Across Every Discipline

Qatoto unites diverse problem solvers on a single shared ledger, pairing the distinct needs of each discipline with platform-enforced value:

### 1. Founders & Hardware Entrepreneurs

- **Need**: You have a physical product concept and the drive to build, but lack seed capital, co-founders, or manufacturing expertise.
- **Value**: Validate consumer demand via civic problem mapping, find verified engineering talent, secure milestone-based funding, and receive automated build tracking without needing prior supply chain knowledge.

### 2. Engineers, Designers & Technical Specialists

- **Need**: You want to contribute your engineering, CAD, or mechanical design skills to high-impact physical builds without getting exploited by empty founder equity promises.
- **Value**: Join vetted teams, negotiate pre-locked Fair Market Rates, earn mathematically guaranteed equity via the Slicing Pie dynamic equity protocol, and build a public portfolio of cryptographically verified effort.

### 3. Software Engineers & Digital Builders

- **Need**: You want to build software, AI models, and firmware that solve urgent, real-world problems facing everyday people, but face opaque sweat-equity negotiations and disconnected project management.
- **Value**: Plug your existing workflow directly into Qatoto via **GitHub, Linear, and Jira**. Have your work objectively evaluated via **Abstract Syntax Tree (AST)** diff complexity analysis and temporal checks, earning mathematically verified dynamic equity slices.

### 4. Researchers, Biologists & Chemical Engineers

- **Need**: Scientific breakthroughs (in clean water, biotechnology, green chemistry, and diagnostics) often stay locked in academic papers because researchers lack software engineers, operational co-founders, or commercialization pathways.
- **Value**: Form open **Research Programmes** (`/research-and-development/programs`), organize hypotheses into interactive scientific branch trees (`branch-tree-layout.ts`), publish `informal_paper` tracks, submit lab datasets, and verify wet-bench experiments using forensic physical receipts to take science from the lab to deployed products.

### 5. Visionaries & Domain Problem Solvers

- **Need**: You observe pressing challenges in everyday civic life (contaminated water, grid failures, food spoilage) and want to build a real solution, but struggle to coordinate multi-disciplinary teams across software, wet-lab science, and hardware.
- **Value**: Identify high-density problem clusters via **Civic Pulse**, assemble cross-disciplinary teams, validate regional purchasing power and feasibility, and lead end-to-end execution on a single transparent ledger.

### 6. Backers, Angels & Investors

- **Need**: Hardware and deep-tech startups frequently fail due to opacity, missed milestones, or premature scaling.
- **Value**: Gain radical visibility through the Daily Update Protocol. Inspect ground-truth engineering diffs, video logs, and hash-chained governance ledgers rather than relying on curated quarterly pitch decks.

### 7. Makers, Creators & Sellers

- **Need**: Distributing hardware prototypes, gathering community feedback, and converting early builds into retail sales.
- **Value**: Publish teardowns and showcases in the **Blueprints Hub**, manage video updates in **Qatoto Studio** (mirroring YouTube Studio), and sell products with single and B2B volume pricing in the **Qatoto Store**.

### 8. Contract Manufacturers, Suppliers & ODMs (Original Design Manufacturers)

- **Need**: Vetting customer designs and receiving clean, unambiguous technical specifications to provide accurate production quotes.
- **Value**: Access structured Bills of Materials (BOM), CAD files, and clear component specifications directly from teams ready for manufacturing runs.

## How the Platform Works (The 5-Stage Pipeline)

```
┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐
│  01. PITCH   │ ──> │ 02. ASSEMBLE │ ──> │  03. RAISE   │ ──> │  04. BUILD   │ ──> │  05. DEPLOY  │
│ Community    │     │ Cross-domain │     │ Milestone-   │     │ Proof of     │     │ Storefront,  │
│ problem/idea │     │ team & rates │     │ backed funds │     │ Effort logs  │     │ field & ODM  │
└──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘
```

1. **Stage 1: Pitch (Public Concept & Problem Definition)**
   A visionary or researcher posts a concept or identifies an active Civic Pulse cluster. The listing outlines the core problem, affected populations, proposed technology (software, chemistry, biology, or hardware), and required skills. No pitch deck or insider introductions needed.

2. **Stage 2: Assemble (Multi-Disciplinary Team Matching)**
   Software engineers, researchers, and operators apply to open roles. Before work starts, the team locks in baseline **Fair Market Rates** (what each person would earn in industry) and signs the dynamic equity agreement.

3. **Stage 3: Raise (Milestone-Backed Capital)**
   Teams can raise capital through community crowdfunding, pre-sales, research grants, or private investment. Capital commitments attach to transparent deliverables and release as verifiable milestones are hit.

4. **Stage 4: Build (Daily Update Protocol & Proof of Effort)**
   The team builds in public view under daily accountability:
    - Builders submit end-of-day (EOD) logs (short video walkthroughs, code PR links, or lab photo receipts).
    - AI extracts structured claims (hours worked, code modules touched, lab experiments conducted).
    - Proof of Effort verifies these claims against GitHub/Linear/Jira diffs, AST complexity metrics, or physical receipt forensics.
    - Slices of dynamic equity are allocated deterministically, backed by a mandatory 24-hour social dispute window.

5. **Stage 5: Deploy (Store, Logistics & Real-World Impact)**
   Validated solutions graduate to deployment:
    - Physical hardware and diagnostic kits transition to batch production via vetted ODMs and launch on the **Qatoto Store**.
    - Software tools and civic apps roll out to end users, backed by community analytics and updates via **Qatoto Studio**.
    - Everyday public problems are resolved, improving community lifestyles.

## Key Features

- **Civic Pulse Problem Map (`/research-and-development/problem-map`)**: Geo-tagged citizen reports clustered by density and urgency with privacy-preserving coordinate quantization.
- **Open Research Programmes (`/research-and-development/programs`)**: Long-horizon scientific tracks featuring interactive hypothesis branch trees, informal papers, and multi-modal asset contributions (`data`, `material`, `equipment`, `expertise`).
- **Startup Feasibility Readouts (`/research-and-development/market-research`)**: Grounded evaluations across 3 independent pillars (Need Density, Purchasing Power via World Bank GDP PPP, and Local Manufacturing / Material Availability via UN Comtrade).
- **Proof of Effort Verification Engine**: Multi-modal claim parsing, Git commit signature validation, Abstract Syntax Tree diff complexity analysis, and physical receipt forensics.
- **Slicing Pie Dynamic Equity Protocol**: Mathematical equity allocation governed by risk-weighted inputs (Cash 4x, Unpaid Labor 2x) with a 24-hour dispute window.
- **Tamper-Evident Governance Ledger (`/research-and-development/governance`)**: SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail of all rate agreements, equity shifts, and compensation statements.
- **Blueprints Hub (`/blueprints`)**: Engineering teardown library with interactive 3D exploded view engine (Three.js / React Three Fiber), working prototype showcases, and manufacturing case studies.
- **Store & Studio (`/store`, `/studio`)**: E-commerce marketplace supporting retail and B2B tiered orders, paired with a creator studio for product video logs, analytics, and earnings.

## How to Start a New Project

```
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Discover Need       │ ───> │ 2. Evaluate Feasibility│ ───> │ 3. Launch Idea Wizard  │
│ Civic Pulse Map &      │      │ World Bank indicators  │      │ /research-and-         │
│ Research Programmes    │      │ & Comtrade trade data  │      │    development/new     │
└────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
                                                                             │
                                                                             ▼
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│ 6. Daily Build Protocol│ <─── │ 5. Lock Rates & Equity │ <─── │ 4. Assemble Team       │
│ Proof of Effort logs   │      │ Dynamic Slicing Pie    │      │ Talent Hub matching    │
│ & AST verification     │      │ agreement              │      │ (software/sci/hardware)│
└────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Discover the Problem**:
   Explore the **Civic Pulse Problem Map** (`/research-and-development/problem-map`) to identify verified pain points reported by citizens, or join an active **Research Programme** (`/research-and-development/programs`) tackling long-horizon biological, chemical, or energy challenges.
2. **Evaluate Market & Technical Feasibility**:
   Open **Market Research** (`/research-and-development/market-research`) to assess local need density, regional purchasing power, and domestic supply chain readiness.
3. **Launch the Idea Wizard** (`/research-and-development/new`):
   Complete the multi-step project wizard:
    - **Idea Basics**: Name, one-line pitch, and category domain (e.g. Water & Sanitation, Healthcare, Energy, Infrastructure).
    - **Problem & Market**: Plain-language problem statement, target region, and demand evidence.
    - **Roles Needed**: Specify cross-disciplinary roles (e.g. Frontend Engineer, Biochemist, Embedded Firmware Dev, Mechanical Designer), commitment level, and initial offered equity range (in basis points).
    - **Review & Submit**: Save as a private draft or publish to open recruitment.
4. **Assemble Your Multi-Disciplinary Team**:
   Browse candidate profiles in the **Talent Hub** (`/research-and-development/talent`). Filter by discipline, availability, and historical verified effort. Propose and lock Fair Market Rates.
5. **Lock Dynamic Equity & Milestones**:
   Agree on project milestones, capital goals, and risk multipliers.
6. **Initiate the Daily Build Protocol**:
   Begin logging daily EOD updates, enabling the AI verification pipeline to track progress, ground claims against GitHub/CAD/lab diffs, and dynamically adjust equity allocations.

## For Contributors, Researchers & Employees

For software engineers, scientists, and domain contributors dedicating their time to meaningful builds:

### Recognition for Your Work

- **Verified Effort Score**: Every hour you contribute is grounded against verifiable digital artifacts (commit hashes, pull requests, AST diffs, Jira/Linear tickets) or physical receipts (lab notes, photos, material orders).
- **Talent Hub Ranking by Effort**: Contributor profiles can be sorted by verified effort (`sort=effort`), giving you global visibility based on proven output rather than resume claims.
- **Permanent Attribution**: Your contributions to software modules, research papers, teardowns, and builds are immutably recorded in the platform's public ledgers and Blueprints Hub.

### Granular Transparency into Daily Effort

- **Claimed vs. Grounded Minutes**: Clear visibility into every daily log. See what was claimed, what artifacts were verified, and how many grounded minutes were awarded.
- **Real-Time Dynamic Equity Dashboard**: Through the Slicing Pie model, your share of the cap table updates dynamically as you contribute:
  $$\text{Individual Equity \%} = \frac{\text{Slices}_{\text{individual}}}{\sum \text{Slices}_{\text{team}}}$$
  $$\text{Slices}_{\text{labor}} = (\text{Unpaid Hours} \times \text{Fair Market Hourly Rate}) \times 2$$
  $$\text{Slices}_{\text{cash}} = \text{Cash Spent} \times 4$$
- **Month-End Compensation Statements**: Detailed gross compensation summaries combining verified cash pay with equity slice allocations, requiring dual countersignatures and frozen into hash-chained records.

### Active AI Optimization Suggestions

- **Proactive Workflow Optimization**: The AI workflow assistant identifies critical path bottlenecks, recommends parallel execution tracks (e.g. running chemical formulation testing alongside companion app development), and flags component alternatives.
- **Full Model Provenance**: Every suggestion explicitly displays the generating model name, prompt version, confidence score (`confidenceBps`), and cited evidence.
- **Contributor Control**: Suggestions are strictly advisory. Team members can freely review, accept, or dismiss suggestions with custom rationale notes.

### Ground-Truth Feasibility Reports

- Inspect unbundled feasibility readouts before committing your unpaid time to an idea:
    - **Need Density (0–30 pts)**: Verified public reports and active geographic clusters.
    - **Purchasing Power (0–25 pts)**: World Bank GDP per capita (PPP) evaluating whether users can sustain product unit economics.
    - **Manufacturing & Materials Readiness (0–25 pts)**: UN Comtrade trade data and domestic ODM availability.
- Protects researchers and engineers from investing months into commercially or practically unviable projects.

## Privacy & Local Self-Hosting Guide

For teams handling proprietary algorithms, confidential wet-lab formulations, or private hardware schematics, Qatoto is designed to be **run and hosted entirely locally**.

### Privacy-by-Design Architecture

- **Single Storage Key**: The frontend stores all client-side state in a single persistent browser key (`qatoto.browser-preferences`). No third-party tracking scripts, hidden fingerprinting beacons, or unsolicited persistent cookies. Erasure is instantaneous via settings.
- **In-Browser Geolocation Quantization**: Under the Civic Pulse map, exact device GPS coordinates are rounded to 3 decimal places (~110 meters) **inside the browser before transmission**. Raw high-precision coordinates never touch the network or server.
- **Automated EXIF & Metadata Scrubbing**: All uploaded build photos, problem submissions, and hardware receipts are stripped of EXIF, GPS, camera serials, and device fingerprints upon ingestion.
- **Prohibition of Emotion/Affect Inference**: In strict compliance with Article 5(1)(f) of the EU AI Act, the Daily Update Protocol extracts only claims, timestamps, and artifact links. Voice sentiment, facial analysis, and emotion detection are structurally prohibited.
- **Mandatory 24-Hour Human Dispute Window**: Automated algorithmic decisions never lock in immediately. Every allocation proposal enters a mandatory 24-hour review window, satisfying GDPR Article 22 human intervention rights.

### Local Setup & Running the Frontend

#### Prerequisites

- **Node.js**: `>= 24.21.0`
- **pnpm**: `>= 12.3.4`

#### 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/vidyesh95/qatoto-frontend.git
cd qatoto-frontend
```

#### 2. Install Dependencies

```bash
pnpm install
```

#### 3. Configure Local Environment

Create a `.env` file in the root directory:

```bash
# URL of your local Express API backend (defaults to http://localhost:8000)
NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://localhost:8000

# Razorpay Test Key for local payment testing (optional)
NEXT_PUBLIC_RAZORPAY_KEY_ID=rzp_test_sample_key

# Enable OpenFreeMap vector basemap for Civic Pulse (no API key or account required)
NEXT_PUBLIC_CIVIC_PULSE_MAPLIBRE=true
```

#### 4. Run the Development Server

```bash
pnpm dev
```

The application will synchronize MapLibre web worker assets and launch the Turbopack server. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### Connecting to the Local Backend

The frontend communicates with the backend via Next.js rewrites defined in `next.config.ts`:

- Any request sent to `/api/*` on port `3000` is transparently proxied to `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` (port `8000`).
- When running in an isolated offline mode without a backend:
    - **Marketing CMS**: Marketing blog and press articles automatically fall back to the built-in `MOCK_BLOGS` and `MOCK_PRESS` datasets in `src/lib/cms.ts`.
    - **Blueprints**: The 3D exploded view engine and blueprint showcases run with local offline geometric fixtures.
    - **Map Tiles**: Civic Pulse vector tiles are served directly from OpenFreeMap over open protocols without user tracking.

## Tech Stack & Architecture Invariants

- **Framework**: Next.js 16 (App Router) with React 19.3
- **Compiler**: React Compiler enabled (`reactCompiler: true`, `turbopackRustReactCompiler: true`)
- **Bundler**: Next.js Turbopack with filesystem caching
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 via `@tailwindcss/postcss`
- **Type Safety**: TypeScript strict mode, Zod 4 for boundary defense
- **Mapping & 3D**: MapLibre GL, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Google Model Viewer
- **State & Data Fetching**: TanStack React Query v5, Context API
- **Linter & Formatter**: Oxlint (type-aware) and Oxfmt
- **Testing**: Vitest (unit/component), Playwright (end-to-end), Tinybench (performance benchmarks)

### Core Architecture Rules

1. **Thin, Untrusted Client**: The frontend is an untrusted presentation layer. All authentication, validation, pricing, and business logic are independently verified by the backend.
2. **Deterministic UI State (Discriminated Unions)**: Components never use loose boolean flags (e.g. `isLoading`, `isError`). States are modeled as discriminated unions rendered with exhaustive `switch` blocks.
3. **Defensive Boundary Parsing**: All network payloads are parsed with Zod before entering application state. No `any` or `as MyType` assertions.
